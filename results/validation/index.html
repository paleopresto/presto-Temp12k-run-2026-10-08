Temperature 12k Validation Report

Validation of this multi-method composite reconstruction against the published Kaufman et al. (2020) Temperature 12k curves (doi:10.1038/s41597-020-0530-7), bundled from the NOAA archive under reference_data/published/. Both curves are anchored to 0 at 100 yr BP (NOAA archival convention) and metrics computed on absolute anomalies so amplitude and offset errors are visible — unlike r/CE on recentered series, which hide them.
